# Conditions

A term that binds the caller: for example what the node does with an instance when its
window of activity closes.

Class: `condition`.

## Places

- `EstimatedCost.conditions`: in a signed offer, a requirement for the caller.
- `Peer.conditions`: an announcement of what the node does now. The offer is the
  authority for one instance.

## Value

The formal gives the form, and what an empty value means.
