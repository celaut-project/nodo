# Settings of a filesystem entry

What the node does with one entry of the tree: for example a directory that is a shared directory.

Class: `setting`.

## Places

`Service.Container.Filesystem.ItemBranch.policies`: a requirement. The formal says if it is valid for a directory, a file, or both.

## Value

The formal of each setting gives the form of its value, its default when it is absent,
and its `place`. See `../REASON.md` for the rules of all the settings.

## Notes

The rules of a share are in `docs/SHARED_FILESYSTEMS.md`. The file here came before `../../REASON.md` (rule 6.4).
